Name a time of day and decide what lights the character. “Late dusk on a blue-tiled terrace, with one amber lantern beside the door” gives the scene two jobs. The sky establishes the time and the lantern lights the face.

OurDream’s prompting guide recommends describing the lighting and composition and removing competing instructions. Its reference-photo help disallows uploads, so use a written brief for an original adult character.

The cool sky and the warm local light identify two parts of the same fictional scene.

## Give the light a place

Try “An adult traveler in an opaque teal wrap gown stands to the left of the lantern. The side of her face nearest the lantern is warmer.” Add the clothing and crop you need. Remove phrases such as “bright midday sun” if they came from an older draft.

## Review the image in two passes

- At thumbnail size, decide whether the sky and highlights together read as dusk.
- At full size, see whether the brightest side of the face makes sense beside the lamp. Check one nearby shadow if both it and the lamp are visible.

If the dusk setting looks right but the highlight appears on the wrong side, clarify the placement while keeping the time and outfit. Saving the working wording beside the finished image gives you one clear detail to carry forward.
