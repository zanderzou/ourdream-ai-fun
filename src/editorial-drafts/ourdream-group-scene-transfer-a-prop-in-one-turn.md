Move one object to a visible place and let the next speaker decide what to do with it. “Rina sets the brass compass in the middle of the table. Nessa’s notebook stays closed. Nessa, what do you notice about the needle?” gives each person a separate job.

The official OurDream help center’s community FAQ describes choosing a responder by portrait or by selecting the character after typing @. Use the visible choice in your current chat to direct the question.

The compass is on the table and within reach. The second character still has a decision to make.

## Establish who holds it now

Use a named person or location instead of “she gives it to her.” Put the current state at the end of the message if the prop moved earlier in the conversation. One sentence is enough: “The compass is on the table beside your notebook.”

## Keep the next action available

Read the response for two details. The intended person should answer, and the prop should stay where you left it until someone moves it. If the story jumps ahead, restate the location and ask the same narrow question. Write down the accepted move only when it matters to the next scene.
