**Account deletion is a permanent account action, but its scope should be read alongside the privacy policy.** OurDream's [deletion guide](https://help.ourdream.ai/en/articles/8717633) describes removal of account access and private account material. Its [privacy policy](https://main.ourdream.ai/terms/privacy-policy) contains an additional public-character retention provision.

The distinction prevents an overly broad promise that one button necessarily removes every public trace or every separately stored copy.

## Understand what you are giving up

The deletion guide says the action cannot be undone and identifies chat history, unpublished characters and unused coins among the affected items. It directs users to the profile page's danger zone. A new account is not described as restoring the old data.

If your only goal is stopping renewal, consider the billing question separately before choosing a permanent data action. We have not submitted a deletion request or tested restoration.

## Read the public-character provision

Section 9 of the privacy policy reserves the ability to preserve characteristics of a sufficiently popular public character and keep it active after account deletion. The policy does not provide a universal numeric threshold in that passage.

Do not infer that your particular character qualifies, or that it necessarily disappears. Ask the provider to explain the applicable scope if public material is part of your concern. A policy statement is evidence of the provider's published terms, not an independent audit of its systems.

## Make a scope list before submitting

| Category | Question to resolve |
| --- | --- |
| Account access | Will the permanent action remove the account you intend? |
| Private account material | What does the deletion confirmation identify? |
| Public character | Does the retention provision apply to this item? |
| Separate device or cloud copies | Which copies do you control outside the provider? |

Keep the list descriptive. You do not need to gather or send additional sensitive content merely to ask about a category. A reference to an existing account item may be sufficient through the official private channel.

## Preserve the request, then verify its outcome

Record when you submitted the request and the confirmation or support reference. Keep an estimate separate from proof of completion. If access still appears possible later, describe the observation accurately rather than repeatedly submitting an irreversible action without knowing its state.

Deleting an account does not operate your device's download folder, another person's screenshot or a separate cloud backup. Review copies under your control independently when relevant.

If the provider reports that certain information is retained, ask which category and purpose apply. Avoid claiming a legal deadline or a universal right from this general guide. The practical outcome is a documented request with a clear scope, especially where public material or separately retained records require an additional explanation.
