OurDream lists creator visibility changes and content-rule decisions among reasons a public character may disappear. Preserve the old link and check what actually happens when you open it from the verified service. A generic landing page leaves the former character's status unresolved.

## Identify what you can still retrieve

Check the title or character identifier you saved alongside the link. If it is your own original character, look for it in your account and inspect its visibility or review state. If another person created it, the link alone gives you no reason to infer why access changed. Keep any private plot outline you authored for the conversation.

## Choose the next writing step

Use the operator's current support route for your own character if its state appears inconsistent. If you continue with a new design, write an original adult character and bring along only the story facts you own. Recheck the audience setting before replacing an old link.
