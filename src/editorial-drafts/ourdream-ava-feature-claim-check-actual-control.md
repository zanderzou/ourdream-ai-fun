Treat an unexpected feature claim in chat as something to verify. OurDream's provider-hosted FAQ explicitly notes that Ava can get details wrong or fabricate features.

A fluent explanation therefore does not establish that a button exists, that an offer is active or that an account balance has changed.

## Identify the exact claim

Suppose a fictional example reply says, “Your account now has a special reward.” Keep that sentence separate from what the actual profile or balance display shows.

Write down the feature name or promised change. Avoid turning a vague statement into a larger assumption such as permanent access to every paid function.

## Check the place where the action belongs

For an account claim, inspect the relevant account display through the normal interface. For a claimed tool, look at the current feature controls and official help.

OurDream's coin guide describes dreamcoins as an account currency used for certain activities. A sentence spoken by a character is not itself a wallet receipt or evidence that currency was credited.

Do not buy a package merely to make an undocumented instruction seem workable. Likewise, do not enter a password or private payment details into a character conversation in response to a generated request.

## Ask one answerable support question

If the claim still conflicts with the current controls, keep the relevant wording, approximate time, client and a concise description of what you actually see. Remove unrelated private conversation details from anything you choose to share.

Ask, for example, whether the named option is currently available on your client or plan. The FAQ points readers to the provider's community support when unsure; inspect the current official support route before sending information.

This guide does not test Ava or determine an individual account's eligibility. Its practical rule is to distinguish the generated statement, the documented feature and the observed account state. Only a real matching state or provider confirmation can settle the specific discrepancy.
