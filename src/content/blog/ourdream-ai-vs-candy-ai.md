<p class="article-lede">OurDream AI and Candy AI both sit in the AI companion category, but the experience you notice first is different. OurDream AI emphasizes character creation and visual imagination, while Candy AI presents a more guided catalogue of companion personalities.</p>

<div class="article-note"><strong>Quick verdict:</strong> OurDream AI is the stronger fit for users who want to shape a distinctive character and build an ongoing story. Candy AI may feel simpler if choosing from ready-made personalities is the priority.</div>

| Comparison point | OurDream AI | Candy AI |

| Customization focus | Appearance, personality, scenario | Personality and relationship style |

## Character creation

OurDream AI is organized around the fantasy of designing someone specific. A useful review should therefore look beyond the number of presets and ask whether personality, backstory, appearance, and conversation tone remain consistent after the first few messages.

Candy AI is often approached as a discovery experience: choose a profile, start a conversation, and refine preferences from there. That can reduce setup time, but it may give advanced character builders fewer reasons to linger.

## Conversation and story continuity

For either platform, test memory with ordinary details rather than sensitive facts. Mention a fictional destination, preferred story genre, or imaginary event and see whether it is recalled later. Good continuity should support a coherent scene without encouraging disclosure of real addresses, financial information, or private relationship details.

## Images and creative media

Media features can change by plan and region. Compare how clearly each service explains credit use, generation limits, export rights, retention, and deletion. A visually impressive result is less useful if the cost is unpredictable or the user cannot remove it from account history.

## Privacy questions to ask before paying

- Is chat history used for model training?
- Can individual conversations and generated images be deleted?
- What happens to data after account deletion?
- Are third-party processors named?
- Is age eligibility clearly stated?

## Which platform should you choose?

Choose OurDream AI when character craft, visual identity, and ongoing fictional scenarios are your main goals. Consider Candy AI when fast discovery and a guided companion interface matter more. In both cases, use fictional details, review current policies, and start with the lowest-cost plan until you understand the limits.
