<p class="article-lede">Both OurDream AI and Candy AI let adults find or shape fictional companions. The useful question is not which service has a character catalogue: both have discovery routes. It is whether the path from a character idea to a sustained conversation and media scene gives you the controls you actually want.</p>

## Two entry paths, not a false binary

OurDream's own overview invites a short description to create a character, then connects chat with images, video and voice. Candy AI's live site presents discovery, Shorts, chat and character creation. Starting with a ready-made character can shorten onboarding; building one yourself can improve fit if you already know the personality, visual style and scenario. Neither approach guarantees a coherent longer conversation.

| Reader task | OurDream AI question | Candy AI question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find a companion quickly | Can you discover a fitting public character without rebuilding it? | Do Discover and Shorts surface a character worth chatting with? |
| Create one from scratch | How precisely do description, voice and visual direction interact? | Which appearance and personality controls are editable later? |
| Extend a story visually | Can the same identity survive image and video scenes? | Are image and video actions available in your current plan? |
| Estimate cost | Which actions consume Dreamcoins beyond standard chat? | Which messages, calls and media actions use allowances or credits? |

## A reproducible character test

Write a brief for an unmistakably adult fictional curator who is planning a late-night museum opening. Specify one motivation, one disagreement and a visual motif. Use the same brief on both services; do not upload a real person's photograph. Ask each character to continue a short scene, change topics, then return to the museum plan. Record contradictions and whether editing the profile repairs them. This is a proposed reader test, not a claimed benchmark from this publication.

If visual continuity matters, request two different safe scenes. Do not score only the prettiest single image. Count retries, note whether the face, clothing cues and setting persist, and check whether a short video is available at all on the plan you would pay for. The provider's promotional examples do not answer your own success rate.

## Budget and privacy before subscription

OurDream's help distinguishes subscriber chat from coin-consuming models, images, video and voice. Candy AI's current plan and media entitlements should be checked on its own checkout. Compare one ordinary week: how many chats, images, clips and retry attempts would you realistically make? Also read both privacy policies for conversation processing, generated media and account deletion. A private-looking chat interface does not mean a legal guarantee of confidentiality.

## Which to choose

Start with OurDream if the core task is moving a custom character through chat and visual storytelling. Consider Candy AI if its Discover/Shorts route or its character controls make finding a companion easier for you. Keep the verdict conditional until you test the same fictional brief and verify current costs and rights on both official sites.
