<p class="article-lede">OurDream AI and Replika answer different companion questions. OurDream asks what fictional characters and scenes you want to create; Replika asks what kind of ongoing AI friend you want to spend time with. Neither premise proves that one app remembers better or protects data more effectively. Those are separate claims to check.</p>

## First decide what you are building

OurDream's official product pages describe character design followed by text conversation, voice and image or video generation. It is a creative workspace for trying more than one persona and scene. Replika's official help describes a personalized companion whose relationship status, conversation style and memories evolve around a continuing interaction. If you want a fresh cast of fictional characters, OurDream's workflow is the relevant starting point. If the same AI friend is the point, Replika's narrower focus may feel less like a production studio.

| Decision | OurDream AI | Replika |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main starting point | Create or select a fictional character | Develop a personal AI companion |
| Typical follow-up | Change scenes, media and sometimes model | Continue one relationship and conversation |
| Visual question | Compare image and video consistency across prompts | Check which avatar, selfie or image features your plan actually includes |
| Cost question | Separate chat access from Dreamcoin-consuming actions | Check Pro/Ultra access and the billing channel |
| Privacy question | What happens to character media and chat history? | Which personal details shape the continuing companion? |

## A fair two-session test

Create one clearly adult fictional persona on OurDream and set a harmless goal, such as preparing an imaginary gallery opening. On Replika, set the relationship mode you actually intend to use and introduce a separate harmless fictional preference. Return after a break. Record what each service recalls without supplying the answer in the prompt. Do not treat an affectionate tone as evidence of long-term memory; ask about the fact directly, and note whether a paid feature was required.

The same script cannot fully erase the products' different purposes. On OurDream, also check whether the character remains recognizable in a second image or short clip and how many attempts that takes. On Replika, check whether its one-companion model makes follow-up easier and whether you can edit or delete the details it learned. These are proposed reader tests, not measurements this publication performed.

## Compare the full cost, not the entry screen

OurDream's help center distinguishes subscription chat, some model responses, images, video and voice by Dreamcoin use. A free entry point is not a promise that every media experiment costs nothing. Replika's subscription help separates plan levels and purchase channels; changing or deleting an app does not necessarily cancel the underlying subscription. Write down a realistic month of chats, images, calls and renewals for each service, then use today's checkout and support pages rather than an archived price in a comparison post.

## Privacy and emotional boundaries

Because Replika is designed around an ongoing personal companion, avoid turning its memory into a store for addresses, health details or other secrets. OurDream poses an additional media-rights question: use only fictional or authorized people and check how generated assets and conversation history can be removed. Read both current privacy policies before entering intimate material. Neither AI is a human confidant or a replacement for professional help.

## Which is the better fit?

Choose OurDream AI if your actual task is designing several fictional adult characters and bringing them into conversation, images and video, and you are comfortable tracking generation costs. Consider Replika if you want one evolving AI friend more than a visual production workflow. The deciding evidence is your own repeatable, privacy-conscious trial and the current provider terms—not a claimed universal winner.
